# Vistora Mobile 1.0.7 (Build 14)

Release date: 7 October 2026

## Google Play Console — What's new

- Added searchable candidate selection when generating offer letters.
- Improved interview feedback validation with a clear mobile-friendly message when feedback is blank.
- Added confirmation prompts before interview actions and feedback submission.
- Interview status now refreshes automatically after completed actions, including rollback, scheduling, rescheduling, and feedback updates.
- Improved recruitment and interview reliability, navigation, and mobile usability.

## App Store — What's New

Build 14 improves the mobile recruitment experience with searchable candidate selection for offer letters, clearer interview feedback validation, confirmation before interview actions, and automatic status refresh after scheduling, rescheduling, rollback, and feedback updates. General reliability and mobile usability have also been improved.
